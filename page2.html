Our workstation.

Our 3,031 m² docking space offers ample room for vessels of every size, with secure, convenient access. Dedicated service areas handle maintenance, fueling, and cleaning, alongside ample storage for accessories and equipment — suited to short-term or long-term parking alike.

Together, we navigate success.

Blue Point Yacht Parking partners with Asprakis Boatyard for hoisting, extending the services we can offer our clients. The partnership means yachts are lifted and launched with the same care and safety standards we hold ourselves to, keeping your vessel in reliable hands from dock to drydock.
